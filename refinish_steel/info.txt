CHANGELOG v3.4: MAKING FUEL UPDATE

RM CORE

MODULE ENGINE

- Sprites for many vanilla and custom objects can now be both injected and tinted live.
- Building, graphics, and plant injections are now supported.
- Modules can now push config pages to RM hud
